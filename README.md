# Perla Bay — Open World Crime

A 3D open-world crime game set in Bahía Perla, a sun-soaked coastal city
built for the [gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01?ref=emjee).
Drive six original cars, run eight story missions with the crew, dodge the
patrol at up to five wanted stars, and own the bay — all in a desktop
browser, from static files, with no network request.

## The city

Bahía Perla is a full street grid on the coast: a beach road, a pier, a
ferris wheel, a casino, a canal with bridges, and a central plaza. Traffic
cruises the grid, palms line the sand, and the sun sets over the sea.

## The game

- **8 story missions** — Celia Mar, Tomas Rey, Nadia Sol and Duarte Ferrer
  send you across the city: first keys, beach runs, ferris deals, casino
  runs, canal crossings, and a final drive to the north gate at four stars.
- **Wanted 0–5 stars** — patrol cars chase, a helicopter circles above
  with a searchlight, and roadblocks wait ahead. Break line of sight to
  cool the heat. Nadia's crew ability drops a star.
- **6 original cars** — Cometa GT, Onda Coupe, Toro Pickup, Brisa Sedan,
  Vela Van, Faro Sport. Each has its own speed, grip and feel.
- **Dense HUD** — mission tracker, dispatch list with unit callsigns and
  distances, weapons and ammo, circular minimap, speed and car condition,
  health and armour, respect rank, and the radio.
- **Radio** — three synth stations (Perla FM 88.4, Boca FM 101.2,
  Onda FM 94.7), engine hum and sirens, all synthesised in WebAudio.
- **Save** — money, finished missions and respect persist in the browser.

## Controls

- **WASD** — move / drive, **SHIFT** — run
- **F** — enter and exit a car (stand next to a parked car)
- **SPACE** — handbrake, **E** — crew ability (drops a wanted star)
- **Q** — next radio station, **M** — mute
- **1–4** — weapons, **P** — pause

## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. No build step, no server,
no network: everything the game needs is vendored under `game/vendor/`
(three.js 0.187.0, `three.module.min.js`).

## Originality
